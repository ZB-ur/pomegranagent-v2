# Duck-call audition assets

Recording: **Soft Duck Quack**, by **OwennewO**, published January 13, 2024.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/OwennewO/sounds/719105/
License: **CC0 1.0** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The source page describes a female duck softly quacking. The publicly available
high-quality MP3 preview was used; no authenticated original was accessed.

Derived assets are mono 24 kHz / 16-bit PCM WAV. The recording was DC-centered,
gently low-pass filtered, trimmed with padding, faded over 8 ms, and attenuated
relative to the accompanying speech with a -12 dBFS peak cap. `softer.wav` is
another 4 dB quieter. No pitch shifting or synthetic animal sound was used.

`voice.wav` is real local Kokoro synthesis of the fictional sentence shown in
the audition page. `prefix.wav` / `suffix.wav` combine it with `soft.wav` and
a 90 ms separation. They are complete pre-rendered clips, not recordings of a
live child conversation. Current voice/rate and source hash are in
`provenance.json`.

These OwennewO clips remain historical audition samples. The user selected the
qubodup soft variant on 2026-09-09; production audio and its attribution are in
[../duck-call/](../duck-call/README.md). The audition page retains both sources.
